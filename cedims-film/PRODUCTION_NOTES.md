# CEDIMS film production notes

- Format: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 1,800 frames (60 seconds).
- Brand: official horizontal and reverse lockup copied from `static/brand/`.
- Product captures: direct 1910–1920 px screenshots from the authenticated live CEDIMS application, taken on 2026-10-28. Teacher loads, upload, tracker, Term 2 calendar, school and district compliance, district archive, and district trend charts are used. The right edge was cropped to remove account names. No screenshots from PDFs, brochures, presentations, or manuals are used.
- Analytics: the implemented `/dashboard/analytics` route redirects to compliance monitoring. The film uses the real `Explore trends` charts within district compliance, preserving displayed values.
- Verification: no working verification result was available in the selected captures, so the film does not depict a result or QR scan.
- Audio: the supplied stereo narration is a local system-voice placeholder. The original score and interface ticks are generated by `make-score.py`. Replace the voice with a Philippine English performance for publication.
- Captions: burned-in text and `captions.srt` match the voice-over script. Timing is approximate and should be aligned to a final recorded narration.
- The captured values are a dated snapshot of the actual application. Confirm external publication rights and privacy review before release.
- The H.264 master uses CRF 8. The delivery MP4 is a copy of this render.
