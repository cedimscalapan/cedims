# Final voice-over

Voice: Kokoro `af_heart`, generated locally at 0.85 speed. The voice is English with a US accent; no Philippine English voice was available in the selected free model.

Instructional records move through many hands. Each week brings teaching loads, lesson plans, deadlines, and follow-ups. When these pieces are scattered, it takes longer to see what is complete, what still needs attention, and where support is needed. CEDIMS brings the work into one shared view for Calapan East District. Teachers begin with their assigned subjects, upload documents, and see the status of every submission. A calendar keeps upcoming weeks visible. School leaders can review progress across their school, while district supervisors can see the wider picture across five schools. From there, they can open the archive, follow a school folder, and examine trends in the data. The path from classroom record to district insight stays connected. That means less searching, clearer follow-up, and more confident decisions. CEDIMS. Clear records. Shared responsibility.

The generated speech uses “C DIMS” to encourage the intended product-name pronunciation. The on-screen name remains CEDIMS.
